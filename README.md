# E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Tableau

# Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Tableau to explore sales trends, product performance and business performance.

# Objectives
- Analyze sales performance.
- Identify sales trends.
- Explore product performance.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

# Tools Used
- Tableau

## Business Questions

This dashboard aims to answer the following business questions:

- How are sales and profit changing over time?
- Which product categories and sub-categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- What is the overall profitability of the business?
- How does product performance vary across categories?

# Key Insights

- **Sales Performance:** The business generated $12.64M in total sales, with sales showing an overall upward trend from 2011 to 2014.
- **Profitability:** Total profit reached $1.47M, with an overall profit margin of 11.61%.
- **Product Performance:** Technology appears to be the leading category in sales contribution, with Phones showing strong performance among sub-categories.
- **Sales Trends:** Sales fluctuated month-to-month but demonstrated an overall growth pattern over the analyzed period.
- **Business Overview:** The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, product performance, profitability, quantity sold, and regional distribution.
